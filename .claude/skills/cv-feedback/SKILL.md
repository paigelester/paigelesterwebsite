The CV lives in `content/cv.ts`. The owner decides every word; your job is critique, then one proposal at a time.

## 1. Review

Rate every item out of 10 against the rubric below.

In the Work Experience section, rate **per bullet**: a table with the bullet (abbreviated), its score, and the one or two things holding it back. Feedback can be to reword a bullet, merge two, split one, extend one with a result, or delete it. Other sections get a score and notes per item.

Close with section-wide issues (tense consistency, ordering, gaps against claims made in the intro).

Done when every bullet in scope carries a score and a reason.

## 2. Revise

Take the feedback in order, **one piece at a time**. For each:

1. Present a single proposal, as:

   > **Old (5/10):** the current sentence
   > **New (7/10):** the proposed sentence

   For a merge or split, show every old sentence and every new one, each with its score. Offer one option; a menu of alternatives is not wanted. If the owner rejects or tweaks it, revise and show the old/new pair again.

   If the new sentence scores below 8, follow it with numbered questions aimed at facts that would lift it to at least 8: each names an angle (e.g. independence, breadth, delivery, retention, recognition) and gives concrete examples of the evidence that would count. Say that one real fact is enough and rough numbers are fine. Skip this when the new sentence already scores 8 or above.

2. Edit `content/cv.ts` only after the owner approves the exact wording shown. If they ask to undo, restore the original text verbatim.
3. Move to the next piece of feedback only when the owner agrees.

Done with a piece when it is applied, or the owner declines it.

## Rubric

- **Outcome-led**: the result opens the bullet; scope and method follow. "Responsible for…" hides the result.
- **Specific**: numbers, named effects, external validation. Real numbers only: when a result needs a figure you lack, ask the owner for it.
- **Durable**: the owner keeps this CV live, so leave out details that will change soon (current product counts, team rosters); prefer wording that stays true.
- **Distinct**: says something the intro and the other bullets do not.
- **Tight**: one idea, one sentence, no nested clauses; about 35 words is the ceiling.
- **Consistent**: tense and voice match the role's other bullets.

## Banned words

The owner has ruled these out; never propose them:

- coaching (and coach, coached)

Never use semicolons: they read as AI-written. Join clauses with a word ("with", "so", "by") or split the bullet.
